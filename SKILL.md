## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is a story-bearing visual key, not a detailed scene. Compress the subject into a few clear shapes: outer contour, structure and mood, built from flat color, simple geometry and a little hand-drawn softness. Keep one core subject and a large breathing field. Reject realistic texture, complex light, a filled page and a pile of secondary objects.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.
